# About FreedomSMP

FreedomSMP is a Minecraft server built around one simple idea: **your world should be yours.**

Whether you're here to build something incredible, explore a growing world, create your own story, compete with other players, or simply hang out with the community, FreedomSMP gives you the freedom to play your way.

## A World That's Yours

FreedomSMP is more than a survival world. It's a place where players can create their own paths, build lasting projects, form alliances, start rivalries, and leave their mark on the server.

There is no single way to experience FreedomSMP. Your adventure can be whatever you make it.

## Build Something That Matters

From massive builds and towns to hidden bases and personal projects, creativity is at the heart of FreedomSMP. Build alone, work with friends, or create something that becomes part of the server's history.

## Adventure, Competition & Chaos

Explore, gather resources, take on challenges, compete with other players, and see where your choices take you. FreedomSMP is designed to leave room for both relaxed gameplay and the unexpected moments that make multiplayer Minecraft memorable.

## Community Comes First

The best Minecraft servers are built by their communities. FreedomSMP is a place to meet people, make friends, collaborate on projects, and create stories together.

Everyone contributes something different to the world, and every player has the opportunity to become part of its history.

## A Custom Minecraft Experience

FreedomSMP includes custom server assets and resource packs designed to give the server its own identity. From custom items and visual enhancements to specialized resource packs, the goal is to make FreedomSMP feel like its own Minecraft experience rather than just another server.

## Freedom Coin

FreedomSMP features the **Freedom Coin**, a custom item created specifically for the server. It is part of the server's custom identity and can be used as the foundation for unique gameplay, rewards, events, and community experiences.

## Growing With the Community

FreedomSMP is an evolving project. New ideas, builds, features, events, and community creations can continue to shape the world over time.

The server is built to grow alongside the people who play on it.

## Your World. Your Story. Your Freedom.

FreedomSMP is about giving players the freedom to create, explore, compete, collaborate, and simply enjoy Minecraft together.

Bring your ideas. Build your story. Make your mark.

**Welcome to FreedomSMP.**
